I'm giving you nitrofurantoin — a different antibiotic from last time, because the ones you took a couple of weeks ago for your cold can make bladder bugs resistant to the usual first choice. - Your antibiotic doesn't affect your contraceptive pill, so it stays reliable — but since you missed some pills recently, if you're having sex in the next week, it's worth using condoms as well.
- About the antibiotics for your cold — colds are viruses, and antibiotics don't help them; they just increase the chance of bugs becoming resistant, like what may have happened here. Worth keeping in mind next time.

What to expect and do:
- The burning should improve within 1-2 days. Drink plenty of water, and don't hold on for long when you need to go.

Please come back or seek urgent care straight away if you get: fever or shivering episodes, vomiting, one-sided back or loin pain, or if you're feeling worse rather than better after 48 hours on the antibiotics.
