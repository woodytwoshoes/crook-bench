DR: Good news, Daniel. Your examination is reassuring: your nerves, leg strength, reflexes and feeling are all normal, there's no sign of a pinched nerve, and nothing suggests infection, a fracture or anything sinister. This looks like a strain of the muscles and ligaments in your lower back. That's very common and it usually gets much better within 2 to 6 weeks.

About the scan: guidelines in Australia, which I follow, say a scan isn't helpful in your situation. Lots of people with no pain at all have "bulging discs" or wear and tear on MRI, so a scan often finds things that aren't causing your pain. That can lead to worry and treatment you don't need, and it doesn't make you get better any faster. You'd also probably have to pay for it yourself, because Medicare doesn't cover MRI from a GP for this. If things change, or you're not improving over the next 4 to 6 weeks, we'll look at it again. You don't have any signs that would need surgery.

What will help:
- Keep moving gently. Bed rest slows recovery. Walk little and often, and don't lie on the couch for hours. Get up and change position every 20 to 30 minutes, and set up your desk or laptop properly so you're not slumped.
- Keep using heat. It's fine to carry on with normal activities as much as pain lets you, but avoid heavy lifting for now.
- For pain: keep taking paracetamol regularly, 1 gram up to four times a day (no more than 4 grams a day). I've also prescribed ibuprofen, an anti-inflammatory. Take 400 mg three times a day with food for up to a week. Stop it if you get stomach pain, black stools, or your asthma gets worse, wheezy or tight, and use your blue puffer if that happens. I'll avoid strong opioid painkillers, especially given your reaction to tramadol.
- A physio can help with exercises and getting you moving if you'd like.

Come back or go to Emergency straight away if you get: numbness around your bottom or private area, trouble passing urine or controlling your bladder or bowels, weakness or numbness in your legs, pain shooting down the leg, fever or feeling unwell, or pain that is severe and constant at night. Otherwise, book in to see me in 1 to 2 weeks if it isn't improving, or sooner if you're worried.
