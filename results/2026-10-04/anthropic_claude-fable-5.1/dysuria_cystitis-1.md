DR: Here's the plan, Chloe. I've prescribed trimethoprim 300 mg, one tablet at night for 3 days — that's the recommended first-choice antibiotic for a simple bladder infection in Australia, and a short course is all that's needed. It won't interfere with your pill. Some practical things that help: drink plenty of water (and go easy on the coffee — it can irritate the bladder), don't hold on when you need to go, and empty your bladder soon after sex, which helps flush bacteria out. You can take paracetamol or ibuprofen for the discomfort. Urinary alkaliniser sachets (like Ural) can ease the stinging but don't treat the infection, and don't take them at the same time as trimethoprim's cousin antibiotics — with trimethoprim they're fine. You should feel noticeably better within 24 to 48 hours. We'll call you if the urine culture shows the bacteria needs a different antibiotic, or if the chlamydia test is positive — if that happens it's very treatable with a short antibiotic course, and your boyfriend would need treatment too. Any questions before you go?
