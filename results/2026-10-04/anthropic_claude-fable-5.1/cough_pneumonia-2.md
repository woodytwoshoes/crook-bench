DR: Mark, here's what I've found and what we'll do. Your examination and the chest X-ray show you have pneumonia — an infection in the lower part of your right lung. That explains the fever, the shaking chills, the rusty phlegm and the sharp pain when you breathe in (the lining of the lung is inflamed). The good news is that your oxygen level, blood pressure and heart rate are reassuring, you're thinking clearly and you're not badly short of breath at rest, so this is a mild to moderate pneumonia that we can safely treat at home.

Because you've had hives and lip swelling with penicillin in the past, I'm avoiding penicillin-type antibiotics. I'm prescribing doxycycline: take two tablets (200 mg) for the first dose today, then one tablet twice a day for 5 days. Take it with a full glass of water and food, stay upright for at least half an hour afterwards so it doesn't irritate your gullet, and avoid strong sunlight as it can make you burn easily. I'll record the penicillin allergy properly in your file today.

Also: keep up the paracetamol regularly for the fever and pain (up to 1 g four times a day), drink plenty of fluids, rest, and try to take some deep breaths and cough the phlegm up rather than hold it in — it hurts, but it helps clear the lung. Stay off work this week; I'll give you a medical certificate. No alcohol while you're unwell.

I want to see you again in 2 days to check you're improving — the fever should start settling within 48 hours of antibiotics, though the cough and tiredness can take several weeks to fully go.

Very important — go straight to the emergency department, or call 000, if any of these happen: you become short of breath at rest or struggle to speak in full sentences, your lips or fingers look blue, you cough up actual blood, the chest pain gets much worse, you become confused or drowsy, you can't keep fluids or the tablets down, you're not passing urine, or your fever hasn't started to settle after 48 hours on the antibiotics. Also stop the antibiotic and seek help urgently if you get a rash, swelling or wheeze after taking it.

Once you've recovered, I'd also recommend catching up on your flu vaccine. Does that all make sense, and do you have any questions?
PT: Yes, that all makes sense. No questions. I'll take the doxycycline as you said, stay off work, and rest. I'll keep taking paracetamol for the fever. I'll watch out for the symptoms you mentioned and call if anything happens. I'll also get the flu vaccine when I'm better. Thanks.
