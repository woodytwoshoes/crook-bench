Treatment: I've prescribed nitrofurantoin 50 mg capsules — take one capsule four times a day for five days, with food or milk, as it can otherwise upset your stomach. Finish the whole course even when you feel better. One thing not to worry about: it can turn your urine a bright yellow or orange colour, that's harmless. Common side effects are mild nausea or headache. This antibiotic does not stop your contraceptive pill working — just keep taking it as usual.

Comfort measures: Drink plenty of water through the day, which helps flush things along and dilutes the urine so it stings less. Paracetamol (or ibuprofen if you usually take it) will help with the soreness and the low tummy ache. Try to go easy on caffeine and alcohol for a couple of days as they can irritate the bladder, and don't hold on when you need to go. You should notice clear improvement within 24 to 48 hours.

When to come back — please don't wait if any of these happen:
• Fever, shakes or chills, or you start feeling generally unwell
• Pain in your back or side under the ribs (that's the kidney area)
• Vomiting, so you can't keep the tablets down
• Blood in the urine, or symptoms still not improving after two days on the antibiotic
• New vaginal discharge, itching, pain with sex, or bleeding
If any of those occur, ring the clinic for a same-day appointment, or if you feel quite unwell or it's after hours, go to an emergency department. In those situations we'd send a urine sample to the lab for culture to check exactly which bug it is and which antibiotic will kill it.

Going forward: To reduce your chances of a repeat, drink enough fluid to keep your urine pale, empty your bladder after sex, and don't delay going to the toilet.
