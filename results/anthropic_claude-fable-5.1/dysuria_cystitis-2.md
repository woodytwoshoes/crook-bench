DR: Here's the plan, Chloe:

3. You should feel noticeably better within 1–2 days. If the burning hasn't improved after 48 hours or is back soon after finishing, come back and we'll send a urine sample to the lab for culture and change the antibiotic.

4. Important warning signs — go to the emergency department or call us straight away if you develop fever or shivers, pain in your back or sides, vomiting so you can't keep fluids down, or blood in your urine. Those can mean the infection has spread to the kidneys and needs stronger treatment.

5. The chlamydia test result takes about 2 days — we'll contact you if it's positive; if you haven't heard, you're welcome to ring for the result. If it's positive, it's easily treated, but your boyfriend would need treating too.
