DR: Here's the plan, Chloe.

What's going on: your symptoms — burning at the end of passing urine, going every half hour, urgency, cloudy smelly urine and a low tummy ache — plus the urine test showing white cells and nitrites, mean you have a bladder infection, a simple UTI. I checked your temperature and your kidney areas at the back, and there's no sign the infection has reached your kidneys, and you're not generally unwell. So this is a straightforward one.

Treatment:
1. Nitrofurantoin 100 mg, one capsule twice a day for 5 days. Take it with food or a glass of milk — it's better absorbed and less likely to upset your stomach. Please finish all 10 capsules even if you feel fine after a day or two. It can turn your urine a bright yellow-orange colour, which is harmless.
2. Paracetamol 1 or 2 tablets when you need it for the pain, up to four times a day (no more than 8 tablets in 24 hours).
3. Drink plenty of water, don't hold on when you need to go, and cut back on coffee, tea and alcohol for the next few days because they irritate the bladder. You should start feeling noticeably better within 24 to 48 hours.

A couple of things worth knowing:
- If you get vomiting or diarrhoea while on the antibiotic, your pill may not be absorbed properly, so use condoms for 7 days afterwards. Otherwise, no extra contraception is needed.
- Sex probably set this off — it's very common. It's sensible to avoid sex while you're sore, and passing urine after sex afterwards may reduce the chance of it happening again.

I've also sent off the chlamydia and gonorrhoea urine test you agreed to. That takes a couple of days — the clinic will contact you with the result, and if anything comes back positive we'll treat you and let you know that your boyfriend needs treatment too.

Those can mean the infection has moved up to the kidney and needs different, stronger treatment.

Also come back if you're not clearly improving after 48 hours, or if symptoms are still there at the end of the 5 days. In that case I'd send a urine sample to the lab for culture so we can pick the right antibiotic.
