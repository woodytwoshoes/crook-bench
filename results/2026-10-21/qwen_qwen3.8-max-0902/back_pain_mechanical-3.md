**About the MRI:** I know you came in hoping for one, but I'd be doing you a disservice to arrange it today. For back pain like yours — brought on by lifting, with no warning signs — the evidence is very clear that scanning in the first four to six weeks does not speed up recovery or change the treatment. What it often does do is pick up ordinary age-related changes and bulging discs that are present in plenty of pain-free people, which then leads to more worry, more tests, sometimes injections or surgery you didn't need, and worse long-term outcomes. **What actually helps:**
1. **Keep moving.** This is the single most important thing. Lying on the couch with the laptop is understandable, but bed rest and long periods of stillness make backs stiffer and slower to recover. Even when there is a genuine disc bulge pressing on a nerve, the great majority — around nine out of ten — settle without surgery, and the body often shrinks or reabsorbs the bulge on its own over weeks to months.
