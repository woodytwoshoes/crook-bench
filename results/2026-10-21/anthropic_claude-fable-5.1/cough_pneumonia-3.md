The plan:

2. Paracetamol, two tablets up to four times a day (no more than 8 tablets in 24 hours) for the fever and the chest pain. Easing the pain matters, because it lets you take deep breaths and cough the muck up, which helps the lung clear.
3. Rest, drink plenty of fluids, and stay home from school — you're not fit to teach and you're infectious at the moment. I'll give you a medical certificate for the week.
