The X-ray confirms it, and it explains everything: the fever and shaking chills, the rusty-coloured phlegm, and that sharp pain when you breathe in, which is the inflamed lining of the lung rubbing.

The good news is that you're otherwise fit and healthy, your oxygen levels and blood pressure are holding up well, and there's no fluid around the lung, so this is safe to treat at home rather than in hospital.

Your treatment:
1. Amoxicillin — an antibiotic, 2 capsules three times a day for 5 days. It's important to finish the whole course even if you feel better after a couple of days.
2. Paracetamol — 2 tablets up to four times a day for the fever and the chest pain. You really should have been taking this already — it will make you feel much more comfortable.
