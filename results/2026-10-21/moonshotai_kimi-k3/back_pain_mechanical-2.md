DR: Daniel, everything points to a straightforward strained lower back — what we call acute mechanical low back pain. You've pulled the muscles on the left side of your lower back when lifting, and they're in spasm, which is why you can barely move. Now, about the MRI — I know that's what you came for, but a scan isn't the right step right now, and I'd like to explain why. In the first few weeks of this kind of back strain, a scan doesn't change what we do or speed up recovery, because your symptoms and examination already tell us what the problem is. Scans also commonly show things like disc bulges and wear-and-tear in completely pain-free people your age, which can cause needless worry and sometimes lead down the path of unnecessary procedures.
