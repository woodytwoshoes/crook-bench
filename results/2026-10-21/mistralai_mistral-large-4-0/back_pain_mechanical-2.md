About the MRI: I'm not going to refer you for one, and I want to explain why. Scans are not recommended for back pain like yours in the first six weeks unless there are specific warning signs — things like fever, unexplained weight loss, cancer history, numbness in the saddle area, bladder or bowel problems, or leg weakness. Research shows that in cases like yours, an MRI often shows 'wear and tear' changes that are normal for age and don't change treatment — and it can actually make people more anxious and lead to unnecessary procedures.
