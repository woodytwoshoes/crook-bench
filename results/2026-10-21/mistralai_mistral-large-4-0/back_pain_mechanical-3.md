DR: Thanks Daniel. This sounds like mechanical low back pain — a muscle and ligament strain on the left side after lifting — rather than a nerve problem or anything serious. Your examination was reassuring: normal temperature and observations, no weakness or numbness in the legs, normal reflexes, and no signs of nerve compression. I found no red flags for fracture, infection, cancer, or cauda equina. Because of that, an MRI is not recommended right now: for recent back pain like this, early scans usually do not change treatment and can show normal age-related changes. Most people improve over 1–2 weeks, though some symptoms can last up to 6 weeks.
