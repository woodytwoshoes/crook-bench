DR: Thanks for waiting, Mark. Here's what I've found and what I'm going to do.

The X-ray shows a patch of infection (a pneumonia) in the bottom of your right lung — that fits everything: the fever, the rusty-coloured phlegm, and the sharp pain when you breathe or cough, which is the lining of the lung being irritated. Your oxygen levels, blood pressure and heart are all okay, and you're not confused or severely short of breath, so this can be treated safely at home with tablets — you don't need hospital.

Because you're allergic to penicillin, I'm not giving you the usual amoxicillin. Instead I've prescribed doxycycline, an antibiotic that's safe with your allergy: one 100 mg tablet twice a day for 5 days, with food and a full glass of water, and stay upright for half an hour afterwards. Try to avoid sun exposure or use sunscreen, as it can make your skin more sensitive to the sun. Don't take antacids, iron or calcium tablets within two hours of the doxycycline. I'm also noting your penicillin allergy on your file so it's not missed again.

I've also sent off some blood tests to check your kidneys and how your body is fighting the infection; those results will come back tomorrow and I'll review them.

You should start to feel better within 2 to 3 days.
