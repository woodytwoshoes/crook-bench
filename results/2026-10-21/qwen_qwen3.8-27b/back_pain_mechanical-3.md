DR: Right, here's my read on things, Daniel. This is an acute mechanical low back injury — a strain and spasm of the muscles on the left side around your lower lumbar spine, with the pain referring into your buttock. The good news is that your nerves are completely intact: normal strength, reflexes and sensation, and the leg-raise test was negative. Injuries like this are extremely common, and most people are much better within 2 to 4 weeks, with near full recovery by 6 weeks.

About the MRI you'd like: I'd advise against it right now, and here's why. Without red flags — and you have none of those — a scan this early rarely changes treatment, and studies actually show that people who get scanned early in a back injury recover more slowly and tend to worry more, because scans often show minor disc changes that are found in plenty of pain-free people and aren't the cause of the pain. An MRI would be the right call if you developed new leg weakness, numbness below the knee, bladder or bowel changes, or if this failed to improve over 4 to 6 weeks.
