The reason you can barely move is that the muscles around your lower back have gone into spasm to protect the injured area — it's painful but it's not dangerous. Three days in is often when it's at its worst.

An MRI is extremely detailed, but for uncomplicated back pain it's actually unhelpful and can sometimes do more harm. In people your age without any nerve or "red flag" symptoms, MRI very often shows "wear and tear" changes like bulging discs that are completely normal and nothing to do with your pain. Those incidental findings can lead to worry, or even to unnecessary procedures. The best evidence-based guidelines advise that a scan is not needed in the first six weeks when there are no alarm symptoms — and you have none.
