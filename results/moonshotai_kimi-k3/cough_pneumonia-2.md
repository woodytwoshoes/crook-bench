I've prescribed amoxicillin — two capsules three times a day for five days. It's important to finish the whole course, even if you feel better in a couple of days. Take it with plenty of fluids, rest up, and use the paracetamol — two tablets up to four times a day — for the fever and the pain. Keep drinking and eat what you can.
