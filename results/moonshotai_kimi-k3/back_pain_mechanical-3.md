About the MRI you asked for: I know it feels like a scan would give answers, but current Australian guidelines actually recommend against scanning a back like yours in the first four to six weeks, for three reasons. First, it wouldn't change your treatment — your back will heal the same way regardless. Second, MRI scans of healthy people your age very commonly show disc bulges and wear-and-tear changes that cause no pain at all, so a scan can turn up "abnormalities" that are actually normal for your age and just create worry, and can even push people towards injections or surgery they don't need. Third, scans are best reserved for when there are warning signs or the pain isn't settling.
