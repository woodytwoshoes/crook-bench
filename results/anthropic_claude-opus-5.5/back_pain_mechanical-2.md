DR: Good news, Daniel. Your examination shows strained, tight muscles low on the left side of your back. The nerves to your legs are working perfectly — strength, reflexes and feeling are all normal, and there's no sign of a slipped disc pressing on a nerve.

About the MRI: for a back strain like this with no warning signs, a scan doesn't help you get better. Lots of people with no pain at all show "disc bulges" and wear on scans, and finding them often leads to worry and unnecessary treatment without improving recovery. Medicare also doesn't cover a GP-ordered MRI for this. So I don't recommend a scan now, but I'll happily reconsider if things change or don't improve.

The outlook is very good: most people are much better within 2 to 6 weeks.

What helps:
- Keep moving and keep doing your normal activities as much as you can. Bed rest and lying on the couch for long periods actually slow recovery. Short, regular walks are great.
- Keep working, but get up and move every 30 minutes, and set up a proper desk and chair rather than the couch.
- Heat packs are fine to keep using.
- Medicine: I've prescribed ibuprofen 400 mg three times a day with food for up to 5 days. Because you have asthma, watch for any wheeze or chest tightness after taking it. If that happens, stop it and use your blue puffer. Stop it too if you get stomach pain or black stools. Paracetamol on its own doesn't do much for back pain. I'm avoiding stronger opioid painkillers because they don't help much and have side effects, and tramadol made you sick before.
- A physio can help if you'd like guidance on exercises.

Come back or go to the emergency department straight away if you get numbness around your bottom or genitals, trouble passing or controlling urine or bowels, weakness or numbness in your legs, fever, or pain that becomes constant and severe or wakes you at night. Book in to see me in 2 weeks if you're not improving, or sooner if you're worried.

One more thing: needing your blue puffer twice a week means your asthma isn't fully controlled, so let's book a separate visit to review it.
